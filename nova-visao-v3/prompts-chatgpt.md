# Nova Visão · prompts para o ChatGPT (gpt-image)

## Como usar (3 passos)
1. Abra o ChatGPT, anexe a imagem `referencias-para-chatgpt.png` e cole um dos prompts abaixo.
2. Peça 4 variações e escolha as que mais gostar.
3. Mande as imagens escolhidas para o Claude. Ele vetoriza, junta com a tipografia e monta o sistema.

Parâmetros: gpt-image-2 · modo edição (com a imagem anexada) · size 1024x1024 para símbolo, 1536x1024 para logo completa · quality high · 4 variações.

Observação: a Diniz é vermelha (conferido no site oficial). O laranja da Nova Visão já diferencia a marca. Os prompts usam a Diniz só como referência de construção e pedem uma marca original.

---

## 1 · Símbolo na diagonal, em perspectiva (o mais próximo da Diniz)

```
Image 1 is a reference for CONSTRUCTION PRINCIPLES only. Do not copy or reproduce its mark, colors or letters.
The principles: a one-color rounded-square badge that contains a bold pair of glasses seen in 3/4 perspective, rotated on a diagonal, drawn as one thick continuous knock-out outline that is large enough to be cropped by the badge edges.

Create an original, non-infringing logo SYMBOL ONLY (no text) for "Nova Visão", a friendly Brazilian optical store.
Solid bright orange (#FF5F00) rounded-square badge with a white glasses silhouette tilted about 35 to 45 degrees. Near lens large, far lens foreshortened, one temple arm and the nose bridge visible. Bold, confident outline, heavier on the top of the frame and thinner at the bottom, like real acetate. Give the frame its own personality, not a generic icon: soft rounded-square lenses with a slightly flat top and a small keyhole bridge.
Flat vector style, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no watermark. Plain white background, centered, generous padding.
Make 4 variations that change only the lens shape: round, rounded-square, half-moon, slightly lifted cat-eye.
```

## 2 · Símbolo frontal, com a mesma linguagem das letras

```
Image 1 is a reference for construction principles only (rounded-square badge, bold glasses silhouette). Image 2 is the lettering of my brand: heavy, wide, rounded geometric lowercase with a hand-drawn wavy tilde. Do not copy Image 1's mark.

Create an original logo SYMBOL ONLY (no text) for "Nova Visão", a friendly Brazilian optical store.
Front-view glasses silhouette inside a solid orange (#FF5F00) rounded-square badge, white knock-out shapes.
Build it with the same heavy stroke weight and rounded terminals as the lettering in Image 2. The lenses echo the round "o" of that lettering, and the bridge is the same wavy tilde as the "ã".
Bold, simple and ownable, readable at 16px. Flat vector, no gradients, no shadows, no details like glints or rivets, no watermark. Plain white background, centered.
Make 4 variations: different lens proportions and different bridge shapes.
```

## 3 · Logo completa popular (símbolo + letras)

```
Create an original horizontal logo for "Nova Visão", a friendly Brazilian optical store.
Left: a rounded-square orange (#FF5F00) badge with a bold white glasses silhouette in 3/4 perspective, tilted diagonally, cropped by the badge edges. Right: the lettering "nova visão" in orange, and below it, very small, widely spaced capitals "ÓTICA".
Flat vector, no gradients, no shadows, no watermark. White background, generous padding. Also show the same logo in white on an orange background.
```
