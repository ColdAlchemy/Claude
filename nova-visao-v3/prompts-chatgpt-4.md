# Nova Visão · P1 com composição própria (novas versões)

## Como rodar
1. Abra um **chat novo** no ChatGPT.
2. Anexe **duas imagens**, nesta ordem:
   - `referencias-3-para-chatgpt.png` (as 10 marcas, A a J)
   - `ref-p1-simbolo.png` (o símbolo do P1 de que você gosta)
3. Cole o prompt abaixo e envie.
4. Mande o resultado para o Claude. Não refine no mesmo chat.

## Prompt

```
Image 1 is a board of labeled brand logos (A to J). Image 2 is my favorite symbol, the style I want to keep.
Use Image 1 only as reference and do not copy any logo. A (Diniz) must NOT be resembled: avoid its exact composition, which is a square badge with a big glasses outline on a bottom-left to top-right diagonal, with the near lens cropped by the corner.

KEEP from Image 2, exactly: the solid orange (#FF5F00) badge with a gentle wave on the bottom edge, the thick continuous white outline of the glasses with round lenses, the flat vector style, and the friendly feeling.
CHANGE only the pose and composition of the glasses, so the symbol no longer looks like the Diniz one.

Create 4 original logo symbols (symbol only, no text) for "Nova Visão", a friendly Brazilian optical store, each with a different glasses composition inside the same kind of orange badge:
1. Front view, both lenses fully visible, slightly tilted about 10 degrees, centered.
2. Side profile: one round lens with the temple arm going back, cropped by the badge edge.
3. Glasses seen from above with the temples folded, lying flat, in a slight angle.
4. Front view with the two lenses at different heights, as if the glasses were being put on, with one temple lifting.

Flat vector, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no watermark. White background, centered, generous padding.
```
